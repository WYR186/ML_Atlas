🟦 Naive Bayes

🧠 Key idea

Generative classifier: model P(X, Y) by factoring P(Y) and P(X | Y) under the assumption that, given Y, the features are conditionally independent.

Factorization + MAP prediction

Conditional independence
$$ P(X_1,\ldots,X_d\mid Y)=\prod_{j=1}^{d} P(X_j\mid Y) $$
MAP decision
$$ \hat{y}=\arg\max_y P(Y=y)\prod_j P(X_j\mid y) $$
Log-space implementation
$$ \hat{y}=\arg\max_y\Big[\log P(y)+\sum_j\log P(X_j\mid y)\Big] $$
Avoids underflow when probabilities are tiny.

Gaussian Naive Bayes

Each feature is Gaussian per class
$$ P(X_j\mid Y=y)=\mathcal{N}(X_j;\,\mu_{y,j},\,\sigma_{y,j}^2) $$
When all classes share the same per-feature variance, the decision boundary is linear — a frequent exam point.
⚠️ Pitfalls
  • The conditional-independence assumption is strong; NB still works in practice for many tasks.
  • Use Laplace smoothing for unseen feature values, otherwise a single zero zeroes out the whole product.
✅ Exam focus
  • Write the conditional-independence formula and the MAP rule.
  • Explain when GNB has a linear boundary (shared variance).
  • Justify log-space inference.

🟦 Naive Bayes · 朴素贝叶斯

🧠 Key idea

生成式分类器：建模 $P(X,Y)$（即 $P(Y)$ 与 $P(X|Y)$）。核心假设：给定 $Y$ 时各特征条件独立。

因子分解 + MAP 预测

条件独立分解
$$ P(X_1,\ldots,X_d\mid Y)=\prod_{j=1}^{d} P(X_j\mid Y) $$
MAP 决策
$$ \hat{y}=\arg\max_y \;P(Y=y)\prod_{j} P(X_j\mid Y=y) $$
Log-space 实现
$$ \hat{y}=\arg\max_y\Big[\log P(y)+\sum_{j}\log P(X_j\mid y)\Big] $$
避免概率连乘下溢；考试遇“为什么用 log”就答这个。

Gaussian Naive Bayes

假设每个 $X_j\mid Y$ 高斯
$$ P(X_j\mid Y=y)=\mathcal{N}\!\big(X_j\,;\,\mu_{y,j},\sigma_{y,j}^2\big) $$
若 所有类共享同一方差 $\sigma_j^2$，GNB 决策边界为 线性（这是常见考点）。
⚠️ Pitfalls
  • 条件独立假设强，不代表真实独立 → 但 NB 在很多任务仍有效。
  • 未见特征值需 Laplace 平滑，否则 $P(X_j\mid y)=0$ 让整个乘积归零。
✅ Exam focus
  • 写出 NB 条件独立公式 + MAP 预测式。
  • 解释 GNB 何时是线性边界（共享方差）。
  • 解释为什么用 log-space。
