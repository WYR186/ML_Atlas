📄 Lecture 27

🟪 Policy Gradient · On-policy

Objective
$$ J(\theta)=\mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim \pi_\theta}[R(\tau)] $$
Log-derivative trick (Monte Carlo estimator)
$$ \nabla_\theta J(\theta)\approx \tfrac{1}{n}\sum_i R(\tau_i)\sum_t \nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t) $$
  • On-policy: gradient uses fresh trajectories from the current $\pi_\theta$.
  • Works for continuous actions and complex policies (e.g. robotics).
🧠 Why log-prob?

Increasing $\log \pi_\theta(a\mid s)$ for high-return $(s, a)$ shifts probability mass toward those actions.

🟪 6. Policy Gradient · On-policy

Objective
$$ J(\theta)=\mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim P^{\pi_\theta}}[R(\tau)] $$
Log-derivative Trick（蒙特卡洛估计）
$$ \nabla_\theta J(\theta)\approx \tfrac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}R(\tau_i)\sum_{t=0}^{H}\nabla_\theta\log \pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t) $$
  • On-policy：通常用当前策略采样的最新轨迹更新。
  • 适用：连续动作、复杂策略（如机器人控制）。
🧠 Why log-prob?

提高高回报动作在给定状态下被采样到的概率。

🧩 Example · on/off 判别
  • Policy Gradient：on-policy（梯度期望依赖当前 $\pi_\theta$ 产生的轨迹）。
  • Q-learning：off-policy（目标用 $\max_{a'}$，可用任意行为策略数据）。
