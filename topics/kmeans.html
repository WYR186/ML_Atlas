🎯 K-means Clustering

Within-cluster sum of squares
$$ J=\sum_i\sum_k r_{ik}\|x^{(i)}-\mu_k\|^2,\;r_{ik}\in\{0,1\} $$
Two-step iteration
$$ k^*(i)=\arg\min_k \|x^{(i)}-\mu_k\|^2 $$
$$ \mu_k=\tfrac{1}{N_k}\sum_{i:k^*(i)=k} x^{(i)} $$
⚠️ Local minima

K-means is not guaranteed to find the global optimum. Initialization matters; k-means++ is the usual fix.

✅ Exam focus
  • Run one or two iterations by hand on a small dataset.
  • Distinguish E-step (assignment) from M-step (mean update).

🎯 5. K-means · 聚类

Objective (WCSS)
$$ J=\sum_i\sum_k r_{ik}\|x^{(i)}-\mu_k\|^2,\ r_{ik}\in\{0,1\} $$
Two-Step Iteration
$$ k^*(i)=\arg\min_k\|x^{(i)}-\mu_k\|^2 $$
$$ \mu_k=\tfrac{1}{N_k}\sum_{i:k^*(i)=k}x^{(i)} $$
⚠️ Pitfall · Local Minima

K-means 不保证全局最优；对初始化敏感（k-means++ 是常见缓解）。

✅ Exam focus
  • 会手跑 1–2 次迭代。
  • 能区分 E-step（分配）vs M-step（更新中心）。
