🟩 Q-learning · Model-free, Off-policy

Update rule
$$ Q(s,a)\leftarrow (1-\alpha)Q(s,a)+\alpha\!\big(r+\gamma\max_{a'}Q(s',a')\big) $$
  • Off-policy: target uses $\max_{a'}$, so the data can come from any behavior policy.
  • Convergence: every $(s, a)$ visited infinitely often + a Robbins-Monro learning rate schedule.
⚠️ Pitfalls
  • Calling Q-learning on-policy. It is off-policy.
  • Forgetting that $\max_{a'}$ takes the best next-step action.
  • Insufficient exploration → wrong $Q$.
🧩 One update

$Q(s,a)=2,\,\alpha=0.5,\,r=1,\,\gamma=0.9,\,\max Q'=4$.

$$ \text{TD target}=1+0.9\times 4 = 4.6 $$
$$ Q\leftarrow 0.5\cdot 2 + 0.5\cdot 4.6 = 3.3 $$

🟩 5. Q-learning · Model-free, Off-policy

Update Rule
$$ Q(s,a)\leftarrow (1-\alpha)Q(s,a)+\alpha\!\left(r+\gamma\max_{a'}Q(s',a')\right) $$
  • Off-policy：学习目标用 $\max_{a'}Q(s',a')$，数据可来自其他行为策略。
  • 收敛条件：所有 $(s,a)$ 被充分探索 + 合适学习率。
⚠️ Pitfalls
  • 把 Q-learning 误认为 on-policy（其实 off-policy）。
  • 忘记 $\max_{a'}$ 是“对下一步最优动作取最大”。
  • 探索不足 → 学到错误 Q。
🧩 Example · 一次更新

$Q(s,a)=2,\ \alpha=0.5,\ r=1,\ \gamma=0.9,\ \max_{a'}Q(s',a')=4$：

$$ \text{TD target}=1+0.9\times 4=4.6 $$
$$ Q\leftarrow 0.5\times 2+0.5\times 4.6=3.3 $$
