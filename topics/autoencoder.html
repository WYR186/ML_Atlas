🧩 Auto-encoder

Encoder $z = f(x)$ compresses; decoder $\hat x = g(z)$ reconstructs.

Reconstruction loss
$$ \min_{\theta,\phi}\;\mathcal{L}_{rec}(x,\hat x) $$
$$ \mathcal{L}_{rec}=\|x-\hat x\|_2^2\;(\text{MSE}) $$
Linear AE ≈ PCA

A single-hidden-layer AE with linear activations is essentially PCA. Adding nonlinearity allows it to learn nonlinear manifolds.

Regularized variants

  • Undercomplete: $\dim(z) < \dim(x)$ (bottleneck).
  • Denoising AE: corrupt input, predict clean $x$.
  • Contractive AE: penalize the encoder Jacobian for robustness.
⚠️ Pitfall

An over-capacity AE without constraints can collapse to the identity map (just memorizes inputs).

🧩 1. Auto-encoder · AE

Encoder-decoder 学习低维表示。Encoder：$z=f(x)$；Decoder：$\hat x = g(z)$。

Reconstruction Loss
$$ \min_{\theta,\phi}\ \mathcal{L}_{rec}(x,\hat x) $$
$$ \mathcal{L}_{rec}=\|x-\hat x\|_2^2\ (\text{MSE}) $$
Nonlinear PCA

线性激活 + 单隐层的 AE ≈ PCA。

加非线性激活（ReLU 等）后可学习更复杂流形。

Regularized AEs（防止恒等映射）

  • Undercomplete：$\dim(z)<\dim(x)$（瓶颈）。
  • Denoising AE (DAE)：输入加噪，预测干净 $x$。
  • Contractive AE (CAE)：惩罚 encoder Jacobian，提升鲁棒性。
⚠️ Pitfall

容量过大且无约束 → AE 可能学到接近“复制粘贴”的恒等映射。
