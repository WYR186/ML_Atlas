🎒 Bagging (Bootstrap Aggregating)

  • Train many models in parallel on bootstrap-resampled data, then average.
  • Effect: reduces variance; bias stays roughly the same.
  • Random Forest = bagging + per-split feature subsampling.
✅ Comparison cheat sheet

"Bagging vs Boosting" — answer with three things: training style (parallel vs sequential), bias / variance impact, sensitivity to noise.

🎒 2. Bagging · 装袋法

  • Bootstrap（有放回抽样）独立训练多个模型 → 平均。
  • 主要 降低方差；典型考点：bagging 通常不降低偏差（期望预测器基本不变）。
  • 典型应用：Random Forest（bagging + 特征随机）。

“bagging vs boosting” 答：训练方式（并行 vs 串行）+ 偏差/方差影响 + 噪声敏感性。
