⚔️ Support Vector Machine

Find the separating hyperplane that maximizes the margin.

Hard-margin (linearly separable)
$$ \min_{w,b}\tfrac{1}{2}\|w\|_2^2,\;y_i(w^\top x_i+b)\ge 1 $$
Soft-margin + slack
$$ \min_{w,b,\xi}\tfrac{1}{2}\|w\|^2+C\sum_i\xi_i,\ y_i(w^\top x_i+b)\ge 1-\xi_i,\ \xi_i\ge 0 $$
Hinge loss
$$ \ell_{\text{hinge}}(t)=\max(0,1-t),\quad t=y_i(w^\top x_i+b) $$
📍 Support vectors

Only points on or inside the margin (including misclassified ones) determine the solution. Other points have zero dual coefficients (complementary slackness).

✅ Exam focus
  • "Why does the solution depend only on support vectors?" — non-SV dual coefficients are 0.
  • Compute hinge loss: first $t=y\,f(x)$, then $\max(0,1-t)$.
🧩 Hinge calculation

$y=+1,\ f(x)=0.3$ ⇒ $t=0.3$ ⇒ $\ell_{\text{hinge}}=\max(0,1-0.3)=0.7$.

⚔️ 3. SVM · 支持向量机

找分离超平面 + 让最小间隔最大。

Hard-margin (linearly separable)
$$ \min_{w,b}\ \tfrac{1}{2}\|w\|_2^2 \quad\text{s.t.}\ y_i(w^\top x_i+b)\ge 1 $$
Soft-margin + slack
$$ \min_{w,b,\xi}\ \tfrac{1}{2}\|w\|_2^2 + C\sum_i\xi_i,\ \ y_i(w^\top x_i+b)\ge 1-\xi_i,\ \xi_i\ge 0 $$
Hinge Loss
$$ \ell_{\text{hinge}}(t)=\max(0,1-t),\quad t=y_i(w^\top x_i+b) $$
📍 Support Vectors

只有落在间隔边界上 / 间隔内（含误分）的点决定最终解。其余点的对偶系数为 0（complementary slackness）。

✅ Exam focus
  • “为什么只依赖支持向量？” → 对偶里非支持向量系数 = 0。
  • 会算 hinge loss：先 $t=yf(x)$，再 $\max(0,1-t)$。
🧩 Example · Hinge 计算

$y=+1,\ f(x)=0.3$ ⇒ $t=0.3$ ⇒ $\ell_{\text{hinge}}=\max(0,1-0.3)=0.7$。
