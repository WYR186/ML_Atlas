⚔️ Support Vector Machine

SVM chooses a linear classifier by the max-margin principle. Lecture 7 adds the dual view: every training point receives a Lagrange multiplier, and only support vectors keep nonzero influence.

1. Concept Understanding

For binary labels $y_i\in\{-1,+1\}$, a linear classifier predicts by the sign of $f(x)=w^\top x+b$. If the data are linearly separable, many hyperplanes may classify every point correctly. SVM adds a preference: maximize the margin.

The margin is the distance from the boundary to the closest examples. Those closest examples are the support vectors, because they “support” the final boundary. In the dual, they are exactly the examples with nonzero $\alpha_i$.

2. Plain-English Explanation

Imagine two groups of points with a gap between them. Any wall placed inside the gap separates them, but SVM puts the wall in the middle of the widest possible hallway.

If a noisy point blocks the hallway, soft-margin SVM allows a paid exception through a slack variable. You pay for violations, but you do not let one noisy point destroy the whole classifier.

3. Core Intuition

The raw correctness condition is only $y_i(w^\top x_i+b)>0$. That says the sign is right, but not how safely right it is. SVM asks for a positive buffer: $y_i(w^\top x_i+b)\ge 1$ after fixing the scale of $w$.

Because scaling $w$ and $b$ does not change the decision boundary, SVM fixes the functional margin to 1 and then minimizes $\|w\|$. A smaller $\|w\|$ means a larger geometric margin.

Soft-margin SVM turns this into a practical classifier by trading off margin size against violations through $C$ or $\lambda$.

Duality is not a cosmetic rewrite: it exposes the classifier as a weighted sum of training examples, which is why the next topic can replace inner products with kernels.

4. Key Equations

Signed distance / margin
$$ \gamma(w,b)=\min_i \frac{y_i(w^\top x_i+b)}{\|w\|_2} $$

Symbols. $x_i$ is a training point; $y_i\in\{-1,+1\}$ is its label; $w^\top x_i+b=f(x_i)$ is the signed score before multiplying by $y_i$; $\|w\|_2$ is the Euclidean norm of the normal vector.

How to read it. $y_i f(x_i)$ is positive when the point is correctly classified and larger when it is more confidently correct. Dividing by $\|w\|_2$ converts that functional score into actual distance from the hyperplane.

How to use. Compute the normalized signed distance for each training point, then take the minimum. SVM tries to make this worst-case distance as large as possible.

Hard-margin SVM
$$ \min_{w,b}\frac12\|w\|_2^2 \quad \text{s.t.}\quad y_i(w^\top x_i+b)\ge 1 $$

Symbols. The variables are $w$ and $b$; the constraint must hold for every training example $i$; the right side 1 fixes the scale of $(w,b)$.

How to read it. Minimizing $\|w\|^2$ maximizes the geometric margin because the margin width is proportional to $1/\|w\|$. The constraints say every point must be correctly classified with a safety buffer.

How to use. Use this form only when the data are linearly separable. In derivations, start from this primal and introduce Lagrange multipliers $\alpha_i$ for each margin constraint.

Soft-margin SVM
$$ \min_{w,b,\xi}\frac12\|w\|_2^2+C\sum_i\xi_i $$
$$ y_i(w^\top x_i+b)\ge 1-\xi_i,\qquad \xi_i\ge 0 $$

Symbols. $\xi_i$ is the slack for example $i$; $C$ is the penalty strength for slack; the first term wants a wide margin and the second term pays for violations.

How to read it. $\xi_i=0$ means the point satisfies the margin. $0<\xi_i\le1$ means correct sign but inside the margin. $\xi_i>1$ means the point is misclassified.

How to use. Increase $C$ when you want fewer violations; decrease $C$ when you want more tolerance and a wider, smoother margin.

Hinge-loss form
$$ \ell_{\text{hinge}}(t)=\max(0,1-t),\qquad t=y_i(w^\top x_i+b) $$

Symbols. $t$ is the margin score for one example. The hinge loss is zero only when $t\ge1$, meaning the point is correctly classified and outside the margin.

How to use. For hand calculation, first compute $f(x)$, then $t=yf(x)$, then plug into $\max(0,1-t)$.

Soft-margin dual shape
$$ \max_\alpha \sum_i \alpha_i-\frac12\sum_i\sum_j \alpha_i\alpha_j y_i y_j x_i^\top x_j $$
$$ 0\le \alpha_i\le C,\qquad \sum_i \alpha_i y_i=0 $$

Symbols. $\alpha_i$ is the Lagrange multiplier for example $i$'s margin constraint. $x_i^\top x_j$ is the inner product between training examples $i$ and $j$.

How to read it. Points with $\alpha_i=0$ do not influence the boundary; points with $\alpha_i>0$ are support vectors. The box constraint $0\le\alpha_i\le C$ is what soft margin adds.

How to use. After solving for $\alpha$, predict with $f(x)=\sum_i\alpha_i y_i x_i^\top x+b$. For kernel SVM, replace $x_i^\top x_j$ and $x_i^\top x$ by kernels.

5. Worked Examples

Example A · Hinge loss by hand
Source: Lecture 6 hinge-loss formulation.

If $y=+1$ and $f(x)=0.3$, then $t=yf(x)=0.3$. The hinge loss is $\max(0,1-0.3)=0.7$. The point is correctly classified, but it is inside the margin, so it still pays loss.

Example B · Reading slack variables
Source: Lecture 6 soft-margin interpretation.

If $\xi_i=0$, the point is correctly classified and outside the margin. If $0<\xi_i\le 1$, the sign is still correct but the point lies inside the margin. If $\xi_i>1$, the point is misclassified.

Example C · Soft-margin dual connection
Source: HW2 Problem 2, paraphrased.

Deriving the dual introduces multipliers for the margin and nonnegative-slack constraints. Stationarity gives $w=\sum_i\alpha_i y_i x_i$ and $C-\alpha_i-\mu_i=0$, so $0\le\alpha_i\le C$. Compared with hard-margin SVM, the box constraint is what allows controlled violations.

6. Problem-Solving Tips

Start with the margin score. Compute $t=yf(x)$ before deciding correctness, hinge loss, or slack behavior.
For hard margin, write constraints $y_i f(x_i)\ge 1$ and objective $\frac12\|w\|^2$.
For soft margin, add $\xi_i$ and remember that larger $C$ punishes violations more strongly.
For dual/kernel questions, look for inner products $x_i^\top x_j$; these become kernels $k(x_i,x_j)$.

7. Common Mistakes

  • Confusing functional margin $y_i f(x_i)$ with geometric margin $y_i f(x_i)/\|w\|$.
  • Forgetting that the scale of $w,b$ must be fixed before writing the hard-margin primal.
  • Thinking every correctly classified point has zero hinge loss. Points inside the margin still have positive hinge loss.
  • Interpreting $C$ backward: larger $C$ means violations are more expensive, not cheaper.

8. Exam Focus

  • Derive the hard-margin primal from the max-margin objective.
  • Convert soft-margin constraints into hinge loss.
  • Compute hinge loss and interpret slack values.
  • State why only support vectors determine the classifier.
  • Recognize the soft-margin dual constraint $0\le\alpha_i\le C$.

⚔️ SVM · 支持向量机

SVM 的核心是 max-margin principle：能把训练点分开的 hyperplane 有很多，SVM 选择离最近训练点最远的那一个。Lecture 7 进一步看 dual view：每个训练点得到一个 Lagrange multiplier，最后只有 support vectors 留下非零影响。

1. Concept Understanding · 概念理解

二分类 $y_i\in\{-1,+1\}$ 中，线性分类器看 $f(x)=w^\top x+b$ 的 sign。若数据 linearly separable，很多 hyperplanes 都能全分对。SVM 多加一个偏好：maximize margin。

Margin 是 boundary 到最近样本点的距离。最近的那些点叫 support vectors，因为它们支撑 / 决定最终边界。在 dual 里，它们正是 $\alpha_i$ 非零的样本。

2. Plain-English Explanation · 大白话理解

想象两堆点中间有一条空走廊。随便放一堵墙都可能分开两堆点，但 SVM 会把墙放在最宽走廊的正中间。

如果有噪声点挡住走廊，soft-margin SVM 允许“付费违规”：用 slack variable 记录违规程度，不让一个噪声点毁掉整个分类器。

3. Core Intuition · 核心直觉

普通正确分类只要求 $y_i(w^\top x_i+b)>0$，这只说明 sign 对了，不说明“有多安全”。SVM 要一个 buffer：缩放固定后要求 $y_i(w^\top x_i+b)\ge 1$。

因为同时缩放 $w,b$ 不改变 boundary，SVM 固定 functional margin 为 1，再最小化 $\|w\|$。$\|w\|$ 越小，geometric margin 越大。

Soft-margin SVM 用 $C$ 或 $\lambda$ 在 large margin 和 violations 之间做 trade-off。

Duality 不只是换个写法：它把 classifier 暴露成 training examples 的 weighted sum，所以下一页 kernel method 才能把 inner product 换成 kernel。

4. Key Equations · 核心公式

Signed distance / margin
$$ \gamma(w,b)=\min_i \frac{y_i(w^\top x_i+b)}{\|w\|_2} $$

符号. $x_i$ 是第 $i$ 个 training point；$y_i\in\{-1,+1\}$ 是标签；$w^\top x_i+b=f(x_i)$ 是 score；$\|w\|_2$ 是 normal vector 的 Euclidean norm。

怎么理解. $y_if(x_i)$ 为正表示分类正确，越大表示越有信心。除以 $\|w\|_2$ 后才变成点到 hyperplane 的真实几何距离。

怎么用. 对每个 training point 算 normalized signed distance，然后取最小值。SVM 要 maximize 的就是这个最差点的距离。

Hard-margin SVM
$$ \min_{w,b}\frac12\|w\|_2^2 \quad \text{s.t.}\quad y_i(w^\top x_i+b)\ge 1 $$

符号. 优化变量是 $w,b$；constraint 对每个样本 $i$ 都要成立；右边的 1 是为了固定 $(w,b)$ 的 scale。

怎么理解. 因为 margin width 和 $1/\|w\|$ 成正比，所以 minimize $\|w\|^2$ 等价于 maximize margin。Constraint 表示每个点都要分类正确并留出 buffer。

怎么用. 只在 linearly separable data 上用。推 dual 时，从这个 primal 出发，给每个 margin constraint 加 Lagrange multiplier $\alpha_i$。

Soft-margin SVM
$$ \min_{w,b,\xi}\frac12\|w\|_2^2+C\sum_i\xi_i $$
$$ y_i(w^\top x_i+b)\ge 1-\xi_i,\qquad \xi_i\ge 0 $$

符号. $\xi_i$ 是第 $i$ 个样本的 slack；$C$ 是 slack penalty；第一项想要 large margin，第二项为 violations 付费。

怎么理解. $\xi_i=0$ 表示满足 margin；$0<\xi_i\le1$ 表示 sign 正确但在 margin 内；$\xi_i>1$ 表示 misclassified。

怎么用. 想更少 violations 就增大 $C$；想更宽、更 tolerant 的 margin 就减小 $C$。

Hinge loss form
$$ \ell_{\text{hinge}}(t)=\max(0,1-t),\qquad t=y_i(w^\top x_i+b) $$

符号. $t$ 是一个样本的 margin score。只有 $t\ge1$ 时 hinge loss 为 0，表示点分类正确且在 margin 外。

怎么理解. 分类对但不够安全也会有 loss：$0

怎么用. 手算时先算 $f(x)$，再算 $t=yf(x)$，最后代入 $\max(0,1-t)$。

Soft-margin dual shape
$$ \max_\alpha \sum_i \alpha_i-\frac12\sum_i\sum_j \alpha_i\alpha_j y_i y_j x_i^\top x_j $$
$$ 0\le \alpha_i\le C,\qquad \sum_i \alpha_i y_i=0 $$

符号. $\alpha_i$ 是样本 $i$ 的 margin constraint 对应的 Lagrange multiplier；$x_i^\top x_j$ 是两个 training examples 的 inner product。

怎么理解. $\alpha_i=0$ 的点不影响 boundary；$\alpha_i>0$ 的点是 support vectors。Soft-margin 相比 hard-margin 多出的核心是 box constraint $0\le\alpha_i\le C$。

怎么用. 解出 $\alpha$ 后用 $f(x)=\sum_i\alpha_i y_i x_i^\top x+b$ 预测。Kernel SVM 中，把 $x_i^\top x_j$ 和 $x_i^\top x$ 换成 kernel。

5. Worked Examples · 代表性例题

Example A · 手算 hinge loss
来源：Lecture 6，hinge-loss formulation。

若 $y=+1$ 且 $f(x)=0.3$，则 $t=yf(x)=0.3$。Hinge loss 是 $\max(0,1-0.3)=0.7$。这个点分类正确，但还在 margin 内，所以仍有 loss。

Example B · 读懂 slack variable
来源：Lecture 6，soft-margin interpretation。

若 $\xi_i=0$，点在 margin 外且分类正确。若 $0<\xi_i\le 1$，sign 仍然正确但点在 margin 内。若 $\xi_i>1$，点已经被 misclassified。

Example C · Soft-margin dual 的变化
来源：HW2 Problem 2，题意转述。

推 dual 时，对 margin constraint 和 $\xi_i\ge0$ 分别加 Lagrange multipliers。Stationarity 给出 $w=\sum_i\alpha_i y_i x_i$ 和 $C-\alpha_i-\mu_i=0$，所以 $0\le\alpha_i\le C$。相比 hard-margin，多出来的 box constraint 就是允许可控 violations 的数学形式。

6. Problem-Solving Tips · 解题技巧

先算 margin score。 无论判断对错、hinge loss 还是 slack，都先算 $t=yf(x)$。
Hard-margin： 写 constraints $y_i f(x_i)\ge 1$ 和 objective $\frac12\|w\|^2$。
Soft-margin： 加 $\xi_i$，记住 $C$ 越大，violations 越贵。
Dual / kernel 题： 看到 $x_i^\top x_j$，就知道可以换成 kernel $k(x_i,x_j)$。

7. Common Mistakes · 常见错误

  • 把 functional margin $y_i f(x_i)$ 和 geometric margin $y_i f(x_i)/\|w\|$ 混掉。
  • 忘记先固定 $w,b$ 的 scale，就直接写 hard-margin primal。
  • 以为所有分类正确的点 hinge loss 都是 0；margin 内的正确点仍有 positive loss。
  • 把 $C$ 理解反：$C$ 越大，violations 越贵，不是越便宜。

  • 会把 soft-margin constraints 转成 hinge loss。
  • 会手算 hinge loss，并解释 slack values。
  • 会说明为什么只有 support vectors 决定 classifier。
  • 会认出 soft-margin dual 的 $0\le\alpha_i\le C$。
