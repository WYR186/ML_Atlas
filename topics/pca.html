📉 Principal Component Analysis

PCA is the first unsupervised learning method in Lecture 9. It finds low-dimensional linear directions that preserve as much variance as possible.

1. Concept Understanding

PCA projects unlabeled data into a lower-dimensional subspace. The first principal component is the unit direction with maximum projected variance; later components are orthogonal directions with the next largest variances.

The method solves dimension reduction, data compression, visualization, and feature extraction problems. Common examples include 2D/3D visualization, eigenfaces-style image features, and noise reduction. The crucial first step is centering the data.

2. Plain-English Explanation

Imagine shining a flashlight on a cloud of points and looking at its shadow. PCA chooses the angle where the shadow is spread out the most, because that shadow preserves the most structure.

If you choose a bad angle, many distinct points collapse together. PCA chooses directions that keep the cloud's main shape visible.

3. Core Intuition

Variance measures how much data changes along a direction. A direction with high variance carries more signal about how examples differ from one another.

The optimization becomes an eigenvalue problem: maximizing $w^\top\Sigma w$ subject to $\|w\|=1$ picks the eigenvector of the covariance matrix with the largest eigenvalue. SVD computes the same subspace efficiently in practice.

Two equivalent motivations

Variance view: choose directions where projected data spreads out the most. Reconstruction view: choose a low-dimensional subspace so projecting down and reconstructing back loses as little squared distance as possible. These two views lead to the same principal components.

4. Key Equations

Centering
$$ \mu=\frac1N\sum_i x^{(i)},\qquad \bar x^{(i)}=x^{(i)}-\mu $$

Symbols. $N$: number of data points; $x^{(i)}$: the $i$-th original data vector in $\mathbb{R}^d$; $\mu$: the sample mean, or the center of the point cloud; $\bar x^{(i)}$: the centered version of point $i$

How to read it. Find the center of the point cloud, then rewrite every point as an offset from that center. PCA should describe spread around the mean, not the mean's location.

How to use. For hand problems, average each coordinate separately, subtract that vector from every sample, and only then form the covariance matrix.

Empirical covariance
$$ \Sigma=\frac1N\bar X\bar X^\top=\frac1N\sum_i\bar x^{(i)}\bar x^{(i)\top} $$

Symbols. $\bar X$: matrix whose columns are centered examples $\bar x^{(i)}$; $\Sigma$: covariance matrix; its diagonal entries are variances; $\bar x^{(i)}\bar x^{(i)\top}$: outer product showing how coordinates of one point move together

How to read it. The covariance matrix summarizes the shape of the centered cloud: large diagonal entries mean large variance along coordinates, and off-diagonal entries show coordinates moving together.

How to use. With columns-as-examples, multiply $\bar X\bar X^\top$ and divide by $N$. If your convention uses rows as examples, the equivalent formula is $\bar X^\top\bar X/N$.

First principal component
$$ w_1=\arg\max_{\|w\|_2=1} w^\top\Sigma w $$

Symbols. $w$: a candidate direction vector; $\|w\|_2=1$: unit-length constraint, so scale cannot fake larger variance; $w^\top\Sigma w$: variance of the data after projection onto direction $w$; $\arg\max$: the value of $w$ that makes the expression largest

How to read it. Test every unit direction and choose the one whose projected coordinates have the largest variance. The solution is the top eigenvector of $\Sigma$.

How to use. For a small matrix, solve the eigenvalue problem, sort eigenvalues from largest to smallest, and take the eigenvector attached to the largest eigenvalue as PC1.

Eigenvalues and residual error
$$ |\Sigma-\lambda I|=0,\qquad \Sigma u_k=\lambda_k u_k,\qquad J_L=\sum_{k=L+1}^{d}\lambda_k $$

Symbols. $\lambda_k$ is the variance captured by component $u_k$; $I$ is the identity matrix; $L$ is the number of components kept; $J_L$ is the mean squared reconstruction error from discarding the remaining directions.

How to read it. Eigenvectors give orthogonal principal directions, eigenvalues tell how much variance each direction explains, and discarded eigenvalues are exactly the reconstruction error left behind.

How to use. After sorting $\lambda_1\ge\lambda_2\ge\cdots$, keep the first $L$ vectors and add the rest of the eigenvalues to get the residual error.

Projection and reconstruction
$$ \hat x=U^\top(x-\mu),\qquad \tilde x=U\hat x+\mu $$

Symbols. $U$: $d\times m$ matrix of selected principal directions; $\hat x$: low-dimensional PCA coordinates in $\mathbb{R}^m$; $\tilde x$: reconstructed approximation back in original space

How to read it. Projection records coordinates in the PCA basis after centering. Reconstruction maps those coordinates back into the original feature space and restores the mean.

How to use. Always subtract $\mu$ before multiplying by $U^\top$; always add $\mu$ back after multiplying by $U$.

SVD route
$$ \frac1{\sqrt N}\bar X=USV^\top,\qquad \Sigma=US^2U^\top $$

Symbols. $U$: left singular vectors; PCA directions; $S$: diagonal singular-value matrix; $V^\top$: right singular-vector part; not usually needed for PCA directions; $S^2$: eigenvalues of the covariance matrix under this normalization

How to read it. SVD decomposes the centered data into orthogonal directions and stretch sizes. With the scaled matrix $(1/\sqrt N)\bar X$, the covariance eigenvalues are $s_k^2$; with the unscaled SVD $\bar X=\tilde U\tilde S V^\top$, they are $\tilde s_k^2/N$.

How to use. In high-dimensional data with $d\gg N$, compute the smaller Gram matrix $\bar X^\top\bar X$ of size $N\times N$ first, then recover data-space principal directions from $\bar X v_k$ and normalize.

Step-by-step PCA algorithm
  1. Compute the mean $\mu$.
  2. Center every point: $\bar x^{(i)}=x^{(i)}-\mu$.
  3. Build the covariance matrix $\Sigma$ from centered data.
  4. Solve $|\Sigma-\lambda I|=0$ and find eigenvectors.
  5. Sort by eigenvalue and keep the top $L$ principal components.
  6. Project with $U^\top(x-\mu)$; reconstruct with $U\hat x+\mu$ if needed.

5. Worked Examples

Example A · Two-point first PC
Source: HW2 Problem 6.1, paraphrased.

For $(1,3)$ and $(4,7)$, the mean is $(2.5,5)$. Centered points are $(-1.5,-2)$ and $(1.5,2)$, so the spread lies along $(3,4)$. The unit first PC is $(0.6,0.8)$ up to sign.

Example B · Four-point covariance
Source: HW2 Problem 6.2, paraphrased.

For $(2,0),(2,2),(6,0),(6,2)$, the mean is $(4,1)$. Centering gives combinations of $(\pm2,\pm1)$, so $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}4&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}$. The $x$ direction has larger variance.

Example C · Diagonal covariance
Source: HW2 Problem 6.3, paraphrased.

If $\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(12,6,20,10)$, the largest eigenvalue is 20. The optimal direction is the third standard basis vector, and the optimal objective value is 20.

6. Problem-Solving Tips

Always center first. Most wrong PCA computations start by skipping $\mu$.
For 2D hand problems, compute the covariance matrix, then use eigenvectors.
For diagonal covariance, the answer is the axis with the largest diagonal entry.
For reconstruction questions, project with $U^\top(x-\mu)$ and add $\mu$ back at the end.
If features use different units, standardize to zero mean and unit variance first; otherwise a large-scale feature can dominate PCA.
To choose $L$, inspect explained eigenvalues with a scree plot; profile likelihood is a more automatic option when covered.
Whitening / sphering rescales principal coordinates so the transformed data has identity covariance, removing linear correlation and variance scale.
Variants: Kernel PCA uses the kernel trick for nonlinear variance directions; PPCA treats PCA as a Gaussian latent-variable model and approaches ordinary PCA as noise variance goes to zero.

7. Common Mistakes

  • Forgetting that eigenvector sign does not matter: $w$ and $-w$ span the same component.
  • Using raw data instead of centered data.
  • Confusing rows-as-examples and columns-as-examples conventions when forming $\Sigma$.
  • Thinking PCA uses labels. It is unsupervised.
  • Forgetting that large feature units can dominate unless features are standardized.
  • Mixing SVD normalizations: $\bar X$ gives $\lambda=s^2/N$, while $(1/\sqrt N)\bar X$ gives $\lambda=s^2$.

8. Exam Focus

  • Center a small dataset and compute covariance.
  • Solve the max-variance problem via eigenvectors.
  • Explain the equivalent reconstruction-error minimization view.
  • Project and reconstruct with $U$ and $\mu$.
  • Explain how SVD yields PCA directions.
  • Know residual error is the sum of discarded eigenvalues.

9. Quick Checklist

  • I center data before PCA.
  • I can write $\max_{\|w\|=1}w^\top\Sigma w$.
  • I choose the top eigenvector.
  • Principal components are orthogonal; eigenvalues are variances.
  • I know how to project and reconstruct.
  • I know PCA is truncated SVD on centered data.
  • I know PCA is unsupervised dimension reduction.

📉 PCA · 主成分分析

PCA 是 Lecture 9 的第一个 unsupervised learning 方法：找到低维 linear directions，让投影后的数据尽量保留 variance。

1. Concept Understanding · 概念理解

PCA 把 unlabeled data 投影到低维 subspace。第一主成分是 projected variance 最大的 unit direction；后续主成分是在 orthogonal 约束下继续找剩余最大 variance。

它解决 dimension reduction、compression、visualization、feature extraction 等问题。典型应用包括 2D/3D 可视化、Eigenfaces 风格的图像特征、noise reduction。最关键的第一步是 centering。

2. Plain-English Explanation · 大白话理解

想象你拿手电照一团点云，看它在墙上的影子。PCA 选择那个让影子铺得最开的角度，因为这个影子保留了最多结构。

角度选差了，不同点会挤在一起。PCA 选择能保留数据主要形状的方向。

3. Core Intuition · 核心直觉

Variance 衡量数据沿某个方向变化多少。High-variance direction 通常携带更多样本差异的信息。

优化问题会变成 eigenvalue problem：在 $\|w\|=1$ 下最大化 $w^\top\Sigma w$，会选 covariance matrix 最大 eigenvalue 对应的 eigenvector。实际计算常用 SVD。

两种等价动机

Variance view: 选择投影后 spread 最大的方向。Reconstruction view: 选择一个低维 subspace，让点投影过去再重建回来时 squared distance loss 最小。两种理解最后得到同一组 principal components。

4. Key Equations · 核心公式

Centering
$$ \mu=\frac1N\sum_i x^{(i)},\qquad \bar x^{(i)}=x^{(i)}-\mu $$

符号. $N$: data points 的数量; $x^{(i)}$: 第 $i$ 个原始数据向量，维度是 $d$; $\mu$: sample mean，也就是点云中心; $\bar x^{(i)}$: centered point，表示点相对均值的位置

怎么理解. 先找点云中心，再把每个点改写成“相对中心的偏移”。PCA 要看的是围绕 mean 的 spread，不是 mean 自己在空间里的位置。

怎么用. 手算时每个 coordinate 分别求平均，再从每个 sample 中减掉这个 mean vector，之后才能算 covariance。

Empirical covariance
$$ \Sigma=\frac1N\bar X\bar X^\top=\frac1N\sum_i\bar x^{(i)}\bar x^{(i)\top} $$

符号. $\bar X$: 由 centered examples $\bar x^{(i)}$ 组成的数据矩阵; $\Sigma$: covariance matrix；对角线是各坐标 variance; $\bar x^{(i)}\bar x^{(i)\top}$: outer product，记录一个点各坐标如何一起变化

怎么理解. Covariance matrix 描述 centered cloud 的形状：diagonal entries 是各坐标 variance，off-diagonal entries 表示两个坐标是否一起变大或变小。

怎么用. 若约定 columns 是 examples，就算 $\bar X\bar X^\top/N$；如果题目用 rows-as-examples，则对应写成 $\bar X^\top\bar X/N$。

First principal component
$$ w_1=\arg\max_{\|w\|_2=1} w^\top\Sigma w $$

符号. $w$: 候选 direction vector; $\|w\|_2=1$: unit-length constraint，防止靠放大 $w$ 作弊; $w^\top\Sigma w$: 数据投影到 $w$ 方向后的 variance; $\arg\max$: 让这个值最大的那个 $w$

怎么理解. 在所有 unit directions 里，找 projection variance 最大的方向。解出来就是 $\Sigma$ 的 top eigenvector。

怎么用. 小矩阵题先解 eigenvalue problem，把 eigenvalues 从大到小排；最大 eigenvalue 对应的 eigenvector 就是 PC1。

Eigenvalues and residual error
$$ |\Sigma-\lambda I|=0,\qquad \Sigma u_k=\lambda_k u_k,\qquad J_L=\sum_{k=L+1}^{d}\lambda_k $$

符号. $\lambda_k$ 是第 $k$ 个 component 捕捉到的 variance；$I$ 是 identity matrix；$L$ 是保留的 components 数量；$J_L$ 是丢掉剩余 directions 后的 mean squared reconstruction error。

怎么理解. Eigenvectors 给出互相 orthogonal 的 principal directions，eigenvalues 表示每个方向解释多少 variance，被丢掉的 eigenvalues 加起来就是 reconstruction error。

怎么用. 排好 $\lambda_1\ge\lambda_2\ge\cdots$ 后，保留前 $L$ 个 vectors，剩余 eigenvalues 相加就是 residual error。

Projection and reconstruction
$$ \hat x=U^\top(x-\mu),\qquad \tilde x=U\hat x+\mu $$

符号. $U$: $d\times m$ matrix，列向量是选中的 principal directions; $\hat x$: 低维 PCA coordinates，维度是 $m$; $\tilde x$: 从低维坐标重建回原空间的 approximation

怎么理解. Projection 是在 center 后，用 PCA basis 记录新坐标；reconstruction 是把低维坐标映回原空间，并把 mean 加回去。

怎么用. 投影前一定减 $\mu$；重建后一定加 $\mu$。少掉这一步，答案会整体平移错。

SVD route
$$ \frac1{\sqrt N}\bar X=USV^\top,\qquad \Sigma=US^2U^\top $$

符号. $U$: left singular vectors，也就是 PCA directions; $S$: singular values 组成的 diagonal matrix; $V^\top$: right singular vectors；通常不是 PCA direction 本身; $S^2$: 在这个 normalization 下对应 covariance eigenvalues

怎么理解. SVD 把 centered data 拆成 orthogonal directions 和 stretch sizes。若对 scaled matrix $(1/\sqrt N)\bar X$ 做 SVD，则 covariance eigenvalues 是 $s_k^2$；若对 unscaled $\bar X=\tilde U\tilde S V^\top$ 做 SVD，则 $\lambda_k=\tilde s_k^2/N$。

怎么用. 当 $d\gg N$ 时，先算更小的 Gram matrix $\bar X^\top\bar X$（$N\times N$），再由 $\bar X v_k$ recover data-space principal direction 并 normalize。

PCA 手算流程
  1. 计算 mean $\mu$。
  2. Center 每个点：$\bar x^{(i)}=x^{(i)}-\mu$。
  3. 用 centered data 构造 covariance matrix $\Sigma$。
  4. 解 $|\Sigma-\lambda I|=0$，找 eigenvalues / eigenvectors。
  5. 按 eigenvalue 从大到小排序，保留前 $L$ 个 principal components。
  6. 需要压缩就算 $U^\top(x-\mu)$；需要重建就算 $U\hat x+\mu$。

6. Problem-Solving Tips · 解题技巧

永远先 center。 PCA 错题很多都是少了 $\mu$。
2D 手算： 算 covariance matrix，再找 eigenvectors。
Diagonal covariance： 最大 diagonal entry 对应的 axis 就是答案。
Reconstruction： 先用 $U^\top(x-\mu)$ 投影，最后一定把 $\mu$ 加回去。
Feature units 不同： 先 standardize 到 zero mean / unit variance，否则量纲大的 feature 会 dominate PCA。
选择 $L$： 用 scree plot 看 eigenvalues 的 elbow；如果课程提到 profile likelihood，也可以用它自动找 significant components。
Whitening / sphering： 把 principal coordinates 再缩放到 covariance 为 identity，去掉 linear correlation 和 variance scale。
Variants： Kernel PCA 用 kernel trick 找 nonlinear variance directions；PPCA 把 PCA 写成 Gaussian latent-variable model，noise variance $\sigma^2\to0$ 时接近普通 PCA。

7. Common Mistakes · 常见错误

  • 忘记 eigenvector 的 sign 不重要：$w$ 和 $-w$ 表示同一条 component direction。
  • 用 raw data 直接算 PCA，没有 centering。
  • 混淆 rows-as-examples 和 columns-as-examples 的 covariance 写法。
  • 以为 PCA 用 labels；它是 unsupervised。
  • 忘记 feature scale：不 standardize 时，大单位 feature 可能支配 principal component。
  • 混淆 SVD normalization：对 $\bar X$ 做 SVD 时 $\lambda=s^2/N$；对 $(1/\sqrt N)\bar X$ 做 SVD 时 $\lambda=s^2$。

8. Exam Focus · 考试重点

  • 会 center 小数据集并计算 covariance。
  • 会用 eigenvectors 解 max-variance problem。
  • 能解释 PCA 等价于 minimize reconstruction error。
  • 会用 $U$ 和 $\mu$ 做 projection / reconstruction。
  • 能解释 SVD 如何得到 PCA directions。
  • 知道 residual error 是 discarded eigenvalues 的总和。

  • Principal components 彼此 orthogonal；eigenvalues 表示对应方向 variance。
