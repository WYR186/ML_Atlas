📉 Principal Component Analysis

Reduce dimension while keeping as much variance as possible.

Step 0 · Centering (mandatory)
$$ X_c = X - \mu $$
Without centering, PC1 may pick up "distance from origin" instead of true variance directions.
Empirical covariance
$$ \Sigma = \frac{1}{n}\sum_i x_c^{(i)}\big(x_c^{(i)}\big)^\top $$
First principal component
$$ w_1=\arg\max_{\|w\|=1}\operatorname{Var}(X_c w) $$
Solution: top eigenvector of $\Sigma$.
✅ Exam focus

Often a 2-D problem: center, write $\Sigma$, find the top eigenvector. Don't forget to center first.

🧩 Quick example

Points (1, 3) and (4, 7), $\mu=(2.5, 5)$. Centered: (−1.5, −2), (1.5, 2). PC1 is along [0.6, 0.8].

📉 4. PCA · 主成分分析

在尽量保留方差的前提下降维。

Step 0 · Centering（关键第一步）
$$ X_c = X - \mu $$
不居中：PC1 可能反映“离原点的距离”。
Empirical Covariance
$$ \Sigma = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_c^{(i)}\big(x_c^{(i)}\big)^\top $$
PC1 · 最大方差方向
$$ w_1=\arg\max_{\|w\|=1}\operatorname{Var}(X_c w) $$
解：$\Sigma$ 最大特征值对应的特征向量。
✅ Exam focus

常考 2D：先居中 → 写出协方差矩阵 → 求最大特征值方向。注意必须先居中。

🧩 Example

$(1,3),(4,7)$，$\mu=(2.5,5)$，居中后 $(-1.5,-2),(1.5,2)$，第一主方向 $\propto[0.6,0.8]^\top$。
