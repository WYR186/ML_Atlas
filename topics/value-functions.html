💎 Value & Q-Functions

  • Deterministic policy: $\pi:\mathcal{S}\to\mathcal{A}$.
  • Stochastic policy: $\pi:\mathcal{S}\to\Delta(\mathcal{A})$.
State value
$$ V^\pi(s)=\mathbb{E}\!\left[\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\gamma^t r_t\mid s_0=s,\pi\right] $$
Action value
$$ Q^\pi(s,a)=\mathbb{E}\!\left[\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\gamma^t r_t\mid s_0=s,a_0=a,\pi\right] $$
Relation (deterministic)
$$ V^\pi(s)=Q^\pi(s,\pi(s)) $$
✅ Concept check
  • $V(s)$: how good is this state?
  • $Q(s, a)$: how good is taking action $a$ in state $s$?

💎 2. Policy & Value

策略 $\pi$：

  • 确定性：$\pi:\mathcal{S}\rightarrow \mathcal{A}$
  • 随机性：$\pi:\mathcal{S}\rightarrow \Delta(\mathcal{A})$
State-value
$$ V^\pi(s)=\mathbb{E}\!\left[\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\gamma^t r_t \mid s_0=s,\pi\right] $$
Action-value (Q)
$$ Q^\pi(s,a)=\mathbb{E}\!\left[\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\gamma^t r_t \mid s_0=s,a_0=a,\pi\right] $$
Relation (deterministic policy)
$$ V^\pi(s)=Q^\pi(s,\pi(s)) $$
✅ Concept check
  • $V(s)$：这个状态好不好？
  • $Q(s,a)$：在该状态做这个动作好不好？
