🧭 Optimization

🧠 Core intuition

Optimization tells you how to reach the best parameters. Convexity is the safety net: a PSD Hessian everywhere ⇒ no bad local minima.

Concepts

  • Convex function: bowl-shaped. Local min = global min.
  • PSD / PD Hessian: convex / strictly convex (unique optimum).

Core formulas

Second-order convexity test
$$ \nabla^2 f(w)\succeq 0 \;\Rightarrow\; f \text{ is convex} $$
Gradient descent
$$ w_{t+1}=w_t-\eta\,\nabla f(w_t) $$
Lagrangian
$$ L(w,\lambda)=f_0(w)+\sum_i \lambda_i f_i(w),\ \lambda_i\ge 0 $$
For $\min f_0$ subject to $f_i \le 0$.
Primal vs Dual
$$ \min_w\max_\lambda L \;|\; \max_\lambda\min_w L $$
SVM dual depends only on $x_i^\top x_j$ → kernel trick.
⚠️ Common pitfalls
  • PSD ⇒ convex (possibly non-strict). PD ⇒ strictly convex with a unique optimum.
  • Step size $\eta$ too large diverges; too small converges painfully slowly.
✅ Exam focus
  • Use PSD / PD of the Hessian to argue convexity / uniqueness.
  • Write the Lagrangian for a constrained problem and explain its dual.
🧩 Logistic Hessian PD meaning
  • Hessian shape: $\nabla^2 f(w)\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times d}$, symmetric.
  • PD ⇒ strictly convex ⇒ unique global minimizer; gradient descent with a sensible step size converges cleanly.

🧭 Optimization · 优化

🧠 Core intuition
优化告诉你“怎么走到最好”。凸性保证你不会走错盆地。 Optimization tells you how to reach the best; convexity guarantees no bad local minima.

🔵 概念理解

  • 凸函数 / Convex：像“碗”；局部最小 = 全局最小。
  • PSD / PD（半正定 / 正定）：通过 Hessian 判断曲率。

🟣 核心公式（默写）

Second-order Convexity Test
$$ \nabla^2 f(w)\succeq 0\quad(\text{PSD}) \Rightarrow f\ \text{convex} $$
Gradient Descent
$$ w_{t+1}=w_t-\eta\nabla f(w_t) $$
Lagrangian
$$ L(w,\lambda)=f_0(w)+\sum_i \lambda_i f_i(w),\ \lambda_i\ge 0 $$
用于 $\min f_0(w)$ s.t. $f_i(w)\le 0$。
Primal vs Dual
$$ \text{Primal: }\min_w\max_\lambda L \quad|\quad \text{Dual: }\max_\lambda\min_w L $$
SVM 对偶只依赖内积 $x_i^\top x_j$ → 核技巧。
⚠️ Common pitfalls
  • PSD 只保证凸（可能非严格）；PD 才保证严格凸（唯一最优）。
  • 学习率 $\eta$ 太大：发散；太小：收敛慢。
✅ Exam focus
  • 会用 Hessian 的 PSD/PD 判断凸性与唯一性。
  • 会写带约束问题的 Lagrangian 并解释对偶形式。
🧩 Example · Logistic Hessian PD 含义
  • 形状：$\nabla^2 f(w)\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times d}$（对称）。
  • PD ⇒ 严格凸 ⇒ 唯一全局最小值；GD 在合适学习率下收敛可控。
