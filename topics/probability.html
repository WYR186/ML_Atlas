🎲 Probability

🧠 Core intuition

Probability is the language for reasoning under uncertainty: condition, factor by independence, update via Bayes.

Concepts

  • Conditional probability: probability of A given B has occurred.
  • Independence: knowing B does not change the probability of A.
  • Conditional independence: the central assumption behind Naive Bayes.

Core formulas (memorize)

Conditional Probability
$$ P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A,B)}{P(B)} $$
Bayes' Rule
$$ P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(B\mid A)P(A)}{P(B)} $$
ML slogan: Posterior ∝ Likelihood × Prior
Independence
$$ P(A,B)=P(A)P(B) $$
Conditional Independence
$$ P(A,B\mid C)=P(A\mid C)\,P(B\mid C) $$
Expectation & Variance
$$ \mathbb{E}[X]=\sum_x x\,P(x),\qquad \mathrm{Var}(X)=\mathbb{E}[X^2]-(\mathbb{E}[X])^2 $$
⚠️ Common pitfalls
  • The denominator in conditioning is P(B), not P(A).
  • Independence ≠ conditional independence: variables can be independent overall but dependent given C, or vice versa.
✅ Exam focus
  • Recognize conditional independence in graphical / NB problems and write the factorization.
  • Map "Posterior ∝ Likelihood × Prior" onto concrete variables.
🧩 Worked example · Bayes calculation

Given P(A)=0.3, P(B|A)=0.8, P(B|¬A)=0.2, find P(A|B).

$$ P(B)=0.8\cdot 0.3 + 0.2\cdot 0.7 = 0.38 $$
$$ P(A\mid B)=\frac{0.8\cdot 0.3}{0.38}\approx 0.6316 $$

🎲 Probability · 概率论

🧠 Core intuition
概率论提供“从已知到未知”的推理规则：条件化、独立性、贝叶斯更新。 Probability is the language for reasoning under uncertainty (conditioning, independence, Bayes updates).

🔵 概念理解

  • 条件概率 / Conditional probability：在 $B$ 已发生时，$A$ 发生的概率。
  • 独立 / Independence：知道 $B$ 不会改变 $A$ 的概率。
  • 条件独立 / Conditional independence：Naive Bayes 的核心假设。

🟣 核心公式（默写）

Conditional Probability
$$ P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A,B)}{P(B)} $$
Bayes' Rule
$$ P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(B\mid A)P(A)}{P(B)} $$
ML slogan: Posterior ∝ Likelihood × Prior
Independence
$$ P(A,B)=P(A)P(B) $$
Conditional Independence (Naive Bayes)
$$ P(A,B\mid C)=P(A\mid C)\,P(B\mid C) $$
Expectation & Variance
$$ \mathbb{E}[X]=\sum_x xP(x)\quad\text{or}\quad\int x\,p(x)\,dx,\qquad \mathrm{Var}(X)=\mathbb{E}[X^2]-(\mathbb{E}[X])^2 $$
⚠️ Common pitfalls
  • 条件概率分母是 $P(B)$，不是 $P(A)$。
  • 独立 ≠ 条件独立：可能在总体不独立，但在给定 $C$ 时独立（或反之）。
✅ Exam focus
  • 能在图模型 / 朴素贝叶斯中识别并写出条件独立因子分解。
  • 能把 “Posterior ∝ Likelihood × Prior” 映射到具体变量。
🧩 Example · Bayes 计算

已知 $P(A)=0.3,\ P(B\mid A)=0.8,\ P(B\mid \neg A)=0.2$，求 $P(A\mid B)$。

$$ P(B)=0.8\cdot 0.3+0.2\cdot 0.7=0.38 $$
$$ P(A\mid B)=\frac{0.8\cdot 0.3}{0.38}\approx 0.6316 $$
