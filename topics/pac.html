📐 PAC Learnability & ERM

A class is PAC-learnable if for any target $\epsilon, \delta$ there is a learning algorithm whose output achieves true error $\le \epsilon$ with probability $\ge 1-\delta$, using polynomially many samples.

ERM picks $f \in \mathcal{F}$ minimizing the empirical risk on the training set.

Finite hypothesis class · sample complexity
$$ n \ge \tfrac{1}{\epsilon}\!\Big(\ln|\mathcal{F}|+\ln\tfrac{1}{\delta}\Big) $$
🧩 Plug-in example

$|\mathcal{F}|=1000,\,\epsilon=0.05,\,\delta=0.01$:

$$ n\ge \tfrac{1}{0.05}(\ln 1000+\ln 100)\approx 230.26 $$

Round up to $n \ge 231$.

📐 4. PAC / ERM / 样本复杂度

PAC 可学习：对任意 $\epsilon,\delta$，存在算法在概率 $\ge 1-\delta$ 下输出测试误差 $\le\epsilon$ 的假设，且样本量随参数多项式增长。

ERM：在训练集上选使 $\hat{\epsilon}_D(f)$ 最小的 $f\in\mathcal{F}$。

有限假设空间样本复杂度（必背）
$$ n \ge \tfrac{1}{\epsilon}\!\Big(\ln|\mathcal{F}|+\ln\tfrac{1}{\delta}\Big) $$
🧩 Example · 代入计算

$|\mathcal{F}|=1000,\ \epsilon=0.05,\ \delta=0.01$：

$$ n\ge \tfrac{1}{0.05}\!(\ln 1000+\ln 100)=20\times(6.9078+4.6052)\approx 230.26 $$

取 $n\ge 231$。
