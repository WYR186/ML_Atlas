🚀 Boosting · AdaBoost

Boosting is the sequential ensemble idea at the end of Lecture 8. AdaBoost builds a strong classifier by repeatedly adding weak classifiers that focus on the examples the current ensemble handles poorly.

1. Concept Understanding

AdaBoost is a sequential ensemble method. It combines many weak learners, each only required to perform slightly better than random guessing, into a stronger classifier.

It maintains weights over training examples. Initially all examples are equally weighted. After each weak classifier, misclassified examples receive more weight, so the next weak learner pays more attention to them.

The final classifier is a weighted majority vote: each weak learner predicts, and the more reliable learners receive larger coefficients in the vote.

2. Plain-English Explanation

Boosting is like a tutor who keeps a mistake notebook. Every time you miss a type of question, that question gets circled, highlighted, and shown again in the next practice round.

Easy examples fade into the background; hard examples get louder. The final decision is a weighted committee of all the small tutors.

3. Core Intuition

Bagging reduces variance by averaging independent learners. Boosting reduces bias by combining weak learners sequentially, each one correcting the current ensemble's weak spots.

AdaBoost can be derived as a forward stagewise additive model that minimizes exponential loss. The sample weights are exactly the loss-induced emphasis on examples with low or negative margins $y_iF(x_i)$.

AdaBoost.M1 loop
  1. Initialize. Set every observation weight to $w_i^{(1)}=1/N$.
  2. Fit. Train weak classifier $G_m(x)$ using the current weights.
  3. Measure. Compute the weighted error $err_m$.
  4. Trust. Convert that error into a classifier weight $\alpha_m$.
  5. Reweight. Increase weights on mistakes, normalize, and repeat.
  6. Predict. Output the sign of the weighted vote.

4. Key Equations

Additive ensemble
$$ F_m(x)=F_{m-1}(x)+\alpha_m G_m(x) $$
$$ G(x)=\operatorname{sign}\!\left(F_M(x)\right)=\operatorname{sign}\!\left(\sum_{m=1}^{M}\alpha_mG_m(x)\right) $$

Symbols. $F_m(x)$ is the ensemble score after round $m$. $G_m(x)$ is the weak classifier added at that round. $\alpha_m$ is that classifier's voting weight. $M$ is the total number of rounds.

How to read it. AdaBoost does not average classifiers equally. It adds a signed, weighted vote from each weak learner, then takes the sign of the final score.

How to use. For a new point, compute every weak learner's prediction, multiply by its $\alpha_m$, sum them, and predict $+1$ if the sum is positive and $-1$ if it is negative.

Weighted error
$$ err_m=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N}w_i\,\mathbf{1}\!\left[y_i\ne G_m(x_i)\right]}{\sum_{i=1}^{N}w_i} $$

Symbols. $w_i$ is the current weight of example $i$. $\mathbf{1}[\cdot]$ is an indicator: 1 if the statement is true, 0 otherwise. $N$ is the number of training examples.

How to read it. This is the fraction of total weight placed on the examples that the current weak learner gets wrong.

How to use. Do not count mistakes equally. Add the weights of the misclassified examples, divide by total weight, then use this $err_m$ to compute $\alpha_m$.

Classifier coefficient
$$ \alpha_m=\frac12\log\frac{1-err_m}{err_m} $$

Symbols. $err_m$ is the weighted error of weak learner $m$. $\alpha_m$ is its coefficient in the ensemble. The log compares correct weighted mass $1-err_m$ against mistaken mass $err_m$.

How to read it. If $err_m<0.5$, then $\alpha_m>0$ and the learner is trusted. If $err_m=0.5$, then $\alpha_m=0$. If $err_m>0.5$, $\alpha_m<0$, which effectively flips the learner's vote.

How to use. Compute weighted error first, plug it into the log formula, and watch the edge cases: as $err_m\to0$, $\alpha_m$ becomes very large.

Exponential loss
$$ L(F)=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N \exp(-y_iF(x_i)) $$

Symbols. $N$ is the number of training examples. $y_i\in\{-1,+1\}$ is the true label. $F(x_i)$ is the ensemble score. $y_iF(x_i)$ is the margin: positive means correct with confidence, negative means wrong.

How to read it. Exponential loss punishes low or negative margins heavily, so badly handled examples dominate the next round.

How to use. If $y_iF(x_i)<0$, the example is misclassified and $\exp(-y_iF(x_i))$ is large. Those examples get more attention.

Example weights
$$ w_i^{(m)}=\exp(-y_iF_{m-1}(x_i)) $$

Symbols. $w_i^{(m)}$ is the weight of training example $i$ before round $m$. $F_{m-1}$ is the ensemble before adding $G_m$. A larger $w_i$ means the next weak learner should care more about that example.

How to read it. The weight is an exponential transform of the current margin. Poorly classified points receive large weights.

How to use. Train the next weak classifier using these weights, so it focuses on examples the previous ensemble handled poorly.

Weight update after round $m$
$$ w_i^{(m+1)}\propto w_i^{(m)}\exp\!\left(-\alpha_m y_i G_m(x_i)\right) $$
$$ w_i\leftarrow w_i\exp\!\left(\alpha_m\mathbf{1}[y_i\ne G_m(x_i)]\right),\quad\text{normalize} $$

Symbols. $w_i^{(m)}$ is the old sample weight. $\alpha_m$ is the classifier weight. $y_iG_m(x_i)$ equals $+1$ for a correct prediction and $-1$ for a mistake. $\propto$ means normalize afterward so weights sum to 1.

How to read it. Correct predictions are multiplied by $e^{-\alpha_m}$ and shrink. Mistakes are multiplied by $e^{\alpha_m}$ and grow.

How to use. Update every sample weight, normalize, then train the next weak learner on the new weighted dataset. If the algorithm box uses the indicator version, read it as the same operational rule: mistakes are scaled up before normalization.

Population minimizer of exponential loss
$$ f^*(x)=\frac12\log\frac{\Pr(Y=1\mid x)}{\Pr(Y=-1\mid x)} $$

Symbols. $f^*(x)$ is the best real-valued score under exponential loss. $\Pr(Y=1\mid x)$ and $\Pr(Y=-1\mid x)$ are the class posterior probabilities.

How to read it. The optimal score is half of the log-odds. Positive score means class $+1$ is more likely; negative score means class $-1$ is more likely.

How to use. This explains why the sign of $F(x)$ is a classifier: the sign follows the more probable class.

Weak-edge training bound
$$ L_S(h_T)\le \exp(-2\gamma^2T) $$

Symbols. $L_S(h_T)$ is training error after $T$ rounds. $\gamma$ is the weak learner's edge: each round has $err_t\le 1/2-\gamma$.

How to read it. If every weak learner beats random guessing by a fixed margin, the training error drops exponentially fast with the number of rounds.

How to use. In theory questions, state the edge assumption first, then quote the exponential decay in $T$.

5. Worked Examples

Example A · Lecture 8 boosting scheme
Source: Lecture 8, boosting slide.

Start with a uniform distribution over data. Ask a weak learning oracle for a classifier. Reweight examples where the ensemble is bad. Repeat. This is the high-level AdaBoost loop.

Example B · Why weights focus on mistakes
Source: HW2 Problem 5.2(a-b), paraphrased.

The new loss factors as $w_i\exp(-\alpha_t y_i f_t(x_i))$, where $w_i=\exp(-y_iF_{t-1}(x_i))$. If the current ensemble gets example $i$ wrong, $y_iF_{t-1}(x_i)<0$, so $w_i$ becomes large.

Example C · Deriving the best $\alpha_t$
Source: HW2 Problem 5.2(d-e), paraphrased.

For a fixed weak classifier, the loss becomes proportional to $(1-\epsilon)e^{-\alpha}+\epsilon e^\alpha$. Setting the derivative to zero gives $e^{2\alpha}=(1-\epsilon)/\epsilon$, hence $\alpha^*=\frac12\log((1-\epsilon)/\epsilon)$.

Example D · Viola-Jones face detector
Source: Lecture 8 application context.

Viola-Jones uses AdaBoost to select useful rectangle features from a large pool of simple box filters. The exam point is not image processing detail; it is that boosting can choose and weight many weak feature tests into a fast face detector.

6. Problem-Solving Tips

Identify boosting by: sequential training, reweighted examples, and weighted votes.
When deriving AdaBoost, split sums into correct and incorrect predictions, then express the wrong side as weighted error.
Interpret $\alpha_m$ by error: $err<0.5$ means positive vote, $err=0.5$ means useless, $err>0.5$ means flip or reject.
For theory questions, mention the weak edge $\gamma$ and the exponential training-error bound.
For comparison questions, mention boosting's noise sensitivity and its dependence on previous rounds.
Bagging vs Boosting
Feature Bagging Boosting / AdaBoost
ExecutionParallel, independent modelsSequential, later models depend on earlier mistakes
Data usageBootstrap samplesSame data with adaptive weights
Main effectReduces varianceOften reduces bias and improves margins
Base learnerCan use deep or unstable learnersUsually simple weak learners, such as stumps
NoiseMore robust to noisy labelsMore sensitive to noisy labels and outliers

7. Common Mistakes

  • Calling boosting parallel. It is sequential because later learners depend on earlier mistakes.
  • Forgetting the weak learner is chosen using weighted error, not plain error.
  • Assuming a classifier with $\epsilon>0.5$ should be trusted as-is. It should be flipped or rejected.
  • Forgetting to normalize sample weights after the update.
  • Assuming more boosting rounds are always safer. More rounds increase complexity and can overfit noise.
  • Ignoring noisy labels: boosting can chase them because their weights keep growing.

8. Exam Focus

  • Explain the AdaBoost.M1 loop from uniform weights to weak learner to reweighting.
  • Write the weighted error, classifier coefficient, weight update, and final vote formulas.
  • Derive the factorized exponential loss.
  • Derive $\alpha_t^*=\frac12\log((1-\epsilon_t)/\epsilon_t)$.
  • Know the population minimizer as half log-odds and the weak-edge training-error bound.
  • Remember the bias-complexity trade-off: more rounds lower approximation error but can raise estimation error.
  • Compare bagging and boosting cleanly.

🚀 Boosting · AdaBoost

Boosting 是 Lecture 8 末尾的 sequential ensemble 思想。AdaBoost 通过不断加入 weak classifiers，专门修正当前 ensemble 处理不好的样本，最后组成 strong classifier。

1. Concept Understanding · 概念理解

AdaBoost 是一种 sequential ensemble：把很多只比 random guessing 稍微好一点的 weak learners 组合成一个 strong classifier。

它会维护 training examples 的 weights。初始时每个样本同权；每加入一个 weak classifier，被错分的样本权重变大，下一轮 weak learner 会更关注它们。

最终 classifier 是 weighted majority vote。每个 weak learner 都投票，但更可靠的 learner 会有更大的 coefficient。

2. Plain-English Explanation · 大白话理解

Boosting 像一个拿着错题本的老师。你错过的题会被圈出来、加粗、下一轮继续练。

简单样本慢慢退到背景里；难样本声音越来越大。最后的判断来自一组小老师的 weighted committee。

3. Core Intuition · 核心直觉

Bagging 通过 averaging independent learners 降低 variance；Boosting 通过 sequential correction 降低 bias，让每个新 learner 盯着当前 ensemble 的弱点。

AdaBoost 可以看成 forward stagewise additive model，用一轮一轮加 weak learner 的方式最小化 exponential loss。Sample weights 本质上是在强调 margin $y_iF(x_i)$ 小或为负的样本。

AdaBoost.M1 loop
  1. Initialize. 所有 observation weights 设成 $w_i^{(1)}=1/N$。
  2. Fit. 用当前 weights 训练 weak classifier $G_m(x)$。
  3. Measure. 计算 weighted error $err_m$。
  4. Trust. 把 error 转成 classifier weight $\alpha_m$。
  5. Reweight. 错分样本权重变大，normalize 后进入下一轮。
  6. Predict. 最后输出 weighted vote 的 sign。

4. Key Equations · 核心公式

Additive ensemble
$$ F_m(x)=F_{m-1}(x)+\alpha_m G_m(x) $$
$$ G(x)=\operatorname{sign}\!\left(F_M(x)\right)=\operatorname{sign}\!\left(\sum_{m=1}^{M}\alpha_mG_m(x)\right) $$

符号. $F_m(x)$ 是第 $m$ 轮后的 ensemble score；$G_m(x)$ 是第 $m$ 个 weak classifier；$\alpha_m$ 是它的 voting weight；$M$ 是 boosting rounds 总数。

怎么理解. AdaBoost 不是简单平均 classifiers，而是把每个 weak learner 的 signed weighted vote 加起来，最后看总分正负。

怎么用. 对新点 $x$，先让所有 weak learners 预测，分别乘 $\alpha_m$，求和；总和为正预测 $+1$，为负预测 $-1$。

Weighted error
$$ err_m=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N}w_i\,\mathbf{1}\!\left[y_i\ne G_m(x_i)\right]}{\sum_{i=1}^{N}w_i} $$

符号. $w_i$ 是第 $i$ 个样本当前的 weight；$\mathbf{1}[\cdot]$ 是 indicator，条件成立为 1，否则为 0；$N$ 是 training examples 数量。

怎么理解. 它不是普通 error rate，而是“错分样本占了多少总权重”。难样本权重大，所以错一个难样本代价更高。

怎么用. 把所有错分样本的 weights 加起来，除以总 weight，再用这个 $err_m$ 计算 $\alpha_m$。

Classifier coefficient
$$ \alpha_m=\frac12\log\frac{1-err_m}{err_m} $$

符号. $err_m$ 是第 $m$ 个 weak learner 的 weighted error；$\alpha_m$ 是它在 ensemble 里的 coefficient；log 里比较的是正确权重质量 $1-err_m$ 和错误权重质量 $err_m$。

怎么理解. 若 $err_m<0.5$，则 $\alpha_m>0$，说明 learner 比 random guessing 好；若 $err_m=0.5$，$\alpha_m=0$；若 $err_m>0.5$，$\alpha_m<0$，相当于 flip 它的预测方向。

怎么用. 先算 weighted error，再代入 log。注意边界：$err_m\to0$ 时 $\alpha_m$ 会非常大。

Exponential loss
$$ L(F)=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N \exp(-y_iF(x_i)) $$

符号. $N$ 是 training examples 数量；$y_i\in\{-1,+1\}$ 是真实 label；$F(x_i)$ 是 ensemble score；$y_iF(x_i)$ 是 margin，正值表示分类正确且有信心，负值表示错分。

怎么理解. Exponential loss 会特别惩罚 margin 小或为负的样本，所以这些样本会主导下一轮学习。

怎么用. 如果 $y_iF(x_i)<0$，样本被错分，$\exp(-y_iF(x_i))$ 会很大；下一轮就要更关注它。

Example weights
$$ w_i^{(m)}=\exp(-y_iF_{m-1}(x_i)) $$

符号. $w_i^{(m)}$ 是第 $i$ 个样本在第 $m$ 轮前的 weight；$F_{m-1}$ 是加入新 learner 前的 ensemble；weight 越大，下一轮越需要关心这个样本。

怎么理解. Weight 是当前 margin 的 exponential transform。当前分得越差，weight 越大。

怎么用. 用这些 weights 训练下一轮 weak classifier，让它更努力处理之前分错或低置信度的样本。

Weight update after round $m$
$$ w_i^{(m+1)}\propto w_i^{(m)}\exp\!\left(-\alpha_m y_i G_m(x_i)\right) $$
$$ w_i\leftarrow w_i\exp\!\left(\alpha_m\mathbf{1}[y_i\ne G_m(x_i)]\right),\quad\text{normalize} $$

符号. $w_i^{(m)}$ 是旧 sample weight；$\alpha_m$ 是 classifier weight；$y_iG_m(x_i)=+1$ 表示预测正确，$-1$ 表示预测错误；$\propto$ 表示更新后还要 normalize。

怎么理解. 预测正确时权重乘 $e^{-\alpha_m}$ 变小；预测错误时权重乘 $e^{\alpha_m}$ 变大。

怎么用. 更新所有 sample weights，normalize 到总和为 1，再训练下一轮 weak learner。如果 algorithm box 写成 indicator version，就按同一个操作理解：错分样本先被 scale up，再 normalize。

Population minimizer of exponential loss
$$ f^*(x)=\frac12\log\frac{\Pr(Y=1\mid x)}{\Pr(Y=-1\mid x)} $$

符号. $f^*(x)$ 是 exponential loss 下最优的 real-valued score；$\Pr(Y=1\mid x)$ 和 $\Pr(Y=-1\mid x)$ 是两个 class posterior probabilities。

怎么理解. 最优 score 是 log-odds 的一半。score 为正表示 $+1$ 更可能，为负表示 $-1$ 更可能。

怎么用. 这解释了为什么最终用 $\operatorname{sign}(F(x))$ 做 classifier：sign 对应 posterior 更大的类别。

Weak-edge training bound
$$ L_S(h_T)\le \exp(-2\gamma^2T) $$

符号. $L_S(h_T)$ 是 $T$ 轮后的 training error；$\gamma$ 是 weak learner 的 edge，即每轮满足 $err_t\le 1/2-\gamma$。

怎么理解. 只要每个 weak learner 都稳定地比 random guessing 好一点，training error 就会随 rounds 指数下降。

怎么用. 理论题先写 weak edge assumption，再写 error 随 $T$ 的 exponential decay。

识别 boosting： sequential training、reweighted examples、weighted votes。
推 AdaBoost： 把 sum 拆成 correct predictions 和 incorrect predictions，再把 wrong side 写成 weighted error。

7. Common Mistakes · 常见错误

  • 把 boosting 说成 parallel；它是 sequential，因为后一轮依赖前一轮错误。
  • 忘记 weak learner 是按 weighted error 选择，不是 plain error。
  • 以为 $\epsilon>0.5$ 的 classifier 可以直接信任；通常要 flip 或 reject。
  • 忘记每轮 weight update 后要 normalize。
  • 以为 boosting rounds 越多一定越安全；rounds 增多会提高 complexity，遇到 noise 可能 overfit。
  • 忽略 noisy labels：boosting 会反复追着它们，因为它们的 weights 不断变大。
