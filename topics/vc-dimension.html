🔢 Hoeffding & VC Dimension

Hoeffding (concentration)
$$ \mathbb{P}\!\left(|\bar Z_n-\mathbb{E}[Z]|\le (b-a)\sqrt{\tfrac{\log(2/\delta)}{2n}}\right)\ge 1-\delta $$

VC dimension — the maximum set size that the hypothesis class can shatter. Larger VC ⇒ richer class ⇒ harder to bound.

Typical generalization bound
$$ \epsilon_\mu(f)\le \hat{\epsilon}_D(f)+\mathcal{O}\!\left(\sqrt{\tfrac{d+\ln(1/\delta)}{n}}\right) $$
📜 Fundamental Theorem (binary)

$\mathcal{F}$ is PAC-learnable iff its VC dimension is finite.

🧩 Doubling VC

If $d \to 2d$ at fixed $n$, the bound term scales by $\sqrt{2}$ — looser, more samples needed.

🔢 5. Hoeffding · VC Dimension

Hoeffding（高概率偏差界）
$$ \mathbb{P}\!\left(\big|\bar Z_n-\mathbb{E}[Z]\big|\le (b-a)\sqrt{\tfrac{\log(2/\delta)}{2n}}\right)\ge 1-\delta $$

VC 维度：$\mathcal{F}$ 能打散（shatter）的最大点集大小。VC 越大，模型“能拟合各种标签”越强，复杂度越高。

典型泛化界
$$ \epsilon_\mu(f)\le \hat{\epsilon}_D(f)+\mathcal{O}\!\left(\sqrt{\tfrac{d+\ln(1/\delta)}{n}}\right) $$
📜 统计学习基本定理（必记口径）

二分类中：$\mathcal{F}$ PAC 可学习 ⇔ VC 维 $d$ 有限。

🧩 Example · VC 翻倍

$d\to 2d$，$n$ 不变 ⇒ 泛化项约 $\sqrt{2d/n}=\sqrt{2}\sqrt{d/n}$，变为原来的 $\sqrt{2}$ 倍（界更松）。
