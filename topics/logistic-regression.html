🎯 Logistic Regression

Map a linear score into a probability with the sigmoid.

Probability output
$$ p(y=1\mid x)=\sigma(w^\top x+b),\;\sigma(a)=\tfrac{1}{1+e^{-a}} $$
Linear decision boundary
$$ w^\top x + b = 0 $$
NLL / cross-entropy ($y_i\in\{-1,+1\}$)
$$ \min_w \sum_i \log\!\big(1+\exp(-y_i\,w^\top x_i)\big) $$
🧠 Why logistic over linear for classification?

Probabilistic loss does not over-penalize examples that are already classified correctly with high confidence, unlike MSE.

✅ Exam focus
  • Recognize when a problem screams logistic regression: probability output, log-odds, MLE.
  • "Why is the boundary linear?" — because $\sigma(a)=0.5\Leftrightarrow a=0\Leftrightarrow w^\top x+b=0$.

🎯 2. Logistic Regression · 逻辑回归

把线性打分映射到 $(0,1)$ 概率。

Probability output
$$ p(y=1\mid x)=\sigma(w^\top x+b),\ \sigma(a)=\tfrac{1}{1+e^{-a}} $$
Decision boundary (still linear)
$$ w^\top x + b = 0 $$
NLL / Cross-entropy ($y_i\in\{-1,+1\}$ form)
$$ \min_w\ \sum_{i=1}^{n}\log\!\big(1+\exp(-y_i\,w^\top x_i)\big) $$
🧠 Why logistic over linear for classification?

概率建模更适合分类；对“分得很对”的点不会过度惩罚（不像 MSE 把对的点拽回来）。

✅ Exam focus
  • 看到“概率输出 / log-odds / MLE” → Logistic。
  • “为什么边界线性？”因为 $\sigma(a)=0.5\Leftrightarrow a=0\Leftrightarrow w^\top x+b=0$。
