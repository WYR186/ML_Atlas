🤖 Large Language Models

Modern LLMs are decoder-only transformers trained on next-token prediction.

Autoregressive factorization
$$ P_\theta(X)=\prod_{t=1}^{T} P_\theta(x_t\mid x_{
NLL / cross-entropy
$$ L(X;\theta)=-\tfrac{1}{T}\sum_t \log P_\theta(x_t\mid x_{
Perplexity
$$ PP(X)=\exp(L(X;\theta)) $$
If $L=2$ then $PP=e^2\approx 7.39$.
🧱 BPE tokenization

Iteratively merges frequent symbol pairs to balance vocabulary size against sequence length.

🛠️ Post-training stages
  • Pre-training: huge unlabeled text corpus, next-token loss.
  • SFT: supervised fine-tuning on instruction–response pairs.
  • RLHF: reinforcement learning from human preferences.
  • ICL: in-context learning — no parameter updates, just prompt examples.

🤖 4. Large Language Models · LLM

主流是 decoder-only Transformer，做 next-token prediction。

Autoregressive Factorization
$$ P_\theta(X)=\prod_{t=1}^{T} P_\theta(x_t\mid x_{
NLL / Cross-Entropy
$$ L(X;\theta)=-\tfrac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}\log P_\theta(x_t\mid x_{
Perplexity
$$ PP(X)=\exp\!\big(L(X;\theta)\big) $$
如 $L=2 \Rightarrow PP=e^2\approx 7.39$。
🧱 BPE 分词

迭代合并高频符号对，平衡词表大小与序列长度。

🛠️ Post-training 阶段（区分清楚！）
  • Pre-training：海量无标注文本上的 next-token loss。
  • SFT（Supervised Fine-Tuning）：指令 - 回复对的监督学习。
  • RLHF：人类反馈强化学习（偏好对齐）。
  • ICL（In-context Learning）：不更新参数，仅靠 prompt 示例。
🧩 Example · Mask 必要性

训练时若允许关注未来 token，会信息泄露（“看答案”），训练–推理失配。Causal mask 保证每步只依赖过去。
