🎮 Bellman Optimality贝尔曼最优

V*(s)=max_a Q*(s,a)
📄 Lecture 27

🟧 Bellman Optimality

$$ V^*(s)=\max_a Q^*(s,a) $$
$$ Q^*(s,a)=R(s,a)+\gamma\,\mathbb{E}_{s'\sim P}[V^*(s')] $$
$$ \pi^*(s)=\arg\max_a Q^*(s,a) $$
🧩 Intuition

Optimal value = immediate reward + discounted best future value. The Bellman operator is a $\gamma$-contraction in $\ell_\infty$, guaranteeing a unique fixed point.

🟧 3. Bellman Optimality

$$ V^*(s)=\max_{a\in\mathcal{A}} Q^*(s,a) $$
$$ Q^*(s,a)=R(s,a)+\gamma\,\mathbb{E}_{s'\sim P(\cdot\mid s,a)}\!\left[V^*(s')\right] $$
$$ \pi^*(s)=\arg\max_{a\in\mathcal{A}} Q^*(s,a) $$
🧩 直觉

“最优价值 = 立即奖励 + 折扣后的未来最优价值”。
