🌊 Diffusion Models扩散模型

Forward · Reverse · ELBOForward · Reverse · ELBO
📄 Lecture 20 📝 hw4

1. Concept Understanding

A diffusion model is a generative model that learns to reverse a gradual noising process. The forward process turns clean data $x_0$ into nearly standard Gaussian noise $x_T$; the reverse process starts from noise and denoises step by step back to a sample.

Lecture 20 frames diffusion models as iterative VAEs: instead of one latent variable $z$, we introduce a chain $x_1,\ldots,x_T$ of latent noisy states.

2. Plain-English Explanation

Imagine taking a clear photo and adding a tiny bit of static many times until it becomes pure noise. Training teaches a neural network how to undo one tiny step of static. During generation, you start with random noise and repeatedly clean it up.

That is why image diffusion often looks like coarse-to-fine refinement: early steps recover broad structure; later steps sharpen details.

3. Core Intuition

The forward direction is easy because we choose it: add Gaussian noise according to a schedule. The reverse direction is hard because many clean images could have produced a noisy image. A neural network learns the reverse Gaussian transition, often by predicting the noise or the clean image hidden inside the noisy sample.

The fixed forward process gives a tractable training signal. The learned reverse process becomes a sampler: $x_T\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)$, then $x_T\to x_{T-1}\to\cdots\to x_0$.

4. Key Equations

Notation

$x_0$ is clean data, $x_t$ is the noisy state at step $t$, $T$ is the final almost-pure-noise step, $I$ is the identity covariance matrix, $\beta_t$ is noise variance, $\alpha_t=1-\beta_t$, and $\bar{\alpha}_t=\prod_{i=1}^t\alpha_i$.

Symbols. $x_0$ is clean data, $x_t$ is the noisy state at step $t$, $T$ is the final noise step, $q$ is the fixed forward process, $p_\theta$ is the learned reverse process, and $\alpha_t,\beta_t,\bar\alpha_t$ control one-step and cumulative noise.

How to read it. $q(\cdot)$ is the fixed forward noising process; $p_\theta(\cdot)$ is the learned reverse denoising process. $\alpha_t,\beta_t$ describe one step, while $\bar{\alpha}_t$ describes the cumulative effect from $0$ to $t$.

How to use. first decide whether the question is moving forward ($q$) or backward ($p_\theta$). Then decide whether it asks for one step ($\alpha_t,\beta_t$) or a direct jump from clean data ($\bar{\alpha}_t$).

Forward noising process

$$ q(x_t\mid x_{t-1})=\mathcal{N}\!\left(x_t;\sqrt{\alpha_t}\,x_{t-1},\,\beta_t I\right) $$
$$ x_t=\sqrt{\alpha_t}\,x_{t-1}+\sqrt{\beta_t}\,\epsilon_t,\qquad \epsilon_t\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I) $$

This says: given the previous noisy state $x_{t-1}$, the next state $x_t$ is Gaussian. Its mean is the old state slightly shrunk by $\sqrt{\alpha_t}$, and its covariance is $\beta_t I$, meaning isotropic Gaussian noise of variance $\beta_t$ is added.

  • $\epsilon_t$ is fresh standard Gaussian noise. To sample one step, draw $\epsilon_t$ and plug it into the reparameterized equation.
  • Use the distribution form for ELBO / probability derivations; use the reparameterized form for sampling or deriving $q(x_t\mid x_0)$.

Direct sample from $x_0$

$$ q(x_t\mid x_0)=\mathcal{N}\!\left(x_t;\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}\,x_0,\,(1-\bar{\alpha}_t)I\right) $$

This is the shortcut formula. Instead of simulating every intermediate step, you can jump from clean data $x_0$ directly to any noise level $t$:

$$ x_t=\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0+\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}\epsilon,\qquad \epsilon\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I). $$

When $t$ is small, $\bar{\alpha}_t$ is close to $1$, so $x_t$ is mostly clean signal. When $t$ is large, $\bar{\alpha}_t$ is close to $0$, so $x_t$ is mostly noise.

  • $\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}$ is the remaining signal scale. It shrinks the original clean sample.
  • $\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}$ is the noise scale. It grows as more noise steps accumulate.
  • In training-code style questions, sample $t$, sample $\epsilon$, build $x_t$, then ask the network to recover the noise or clean signal.

Learned reverse transition

$$ p_\theta(x_{t-1}\mid x_t)=\mathcal{N}\!\left(x_{t-1};\mu_\theta(x_t,t),\,\Sigma_\theta(x_t,t)\right) $$

This is the model we learn. At generation time, start from $x_T\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)$, then repeatedly use $p_\theta(x_{t-1}\mid x_t)$ to move from $T$ down to $0$.

  • $\theta$ are neural-network parameters.
  • $\mu_\theta(x_t,t)$ is the predicted mean of the slightly cleaner previous state.
  • $\Sigma_\theta(x_t,t)$ is the reverse-step covariance; in many simplified derivations it is fixed or parameterized simply.

Posterior mean used in HW4 §3.4

$$ \tilde{\mu}_t(x_t,x_0)= \frac{\sqrt{\alpha_t}(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1})}{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}x_t+ \frac{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}\beta_t}{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0 $$

This is the mean of the true posterior $q(x_{t-1}\mid x_t,x_0)$. It is a weighted average of the noisy observation $x_t$ and the clean anchor $x_0$. In derivations, get it from Bayes' rule:

$$ q(x_{t-1}\mid x_t,x_0)\propto q(x_t\mid x_{t-1})q(x_{t-1}\mid x_0). $$

Both factors are Gaussian in the variable $x_{t-1}$, so multiplying them gives another Gaussian. Completing the square gives the mean above.

  • The coefficient on $x_t$ tells how much the current noisy observation pulls the previous step.
  • The coefficient on $x_0$ tells how much the clean reference anchors the posterior.
  • To use the formula, compute $\alpha_t$, $\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}$, $\bar{\alpha}_t$, and $\beta_t$ first; then plug in the two vectors $x_t$ and $x_0$.

5. Worked Examples

HW4 §3.1: write the diffusion ELBO

Source: HW4 §3, paraphrased.

$$ \log p_\theta(x_0)\ge \mathbb{E}_{q(x_1\mid x_0)}[\log p_\theta(x_0\mid x_1)] -D_{KL}(q(x_T\mid x_0)\,\|\,p(x_T)) -\sum_{t=2}^{T}\mathbb{E}_{q(x_t\mid x_0)} \left[D_{KL}(q(x_{t-1}\mid x_t,x_0)\,\|\,p_\theta(x_{t-1}\mid x_t))\right] $$

The three pieces are reconstruction, prior matching, and step consistency.

HW4 §3.3: derive $q(x_t\mid x_0)$

Repeatedly substitute the reparameterized transition. A linear combination of independent Gaussians is Gaussian. The signal coefficient multiplies into $\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}$, and the remaining variance becomes $1-\bar{\alpha}_t$.

HW4 §3.4: posterior mean

Use Bayes' rule:

$$ q(x_{t-1}\mid x_t,x_0)\propto q(x_t\mid x_{t-1})q(x_{t-1}\mid x_0) $$

Both factors are Gaussian in $x_{t-1}$, so their product is Gaussian. Completing the square gives $\tilde{\mu}_t(x_t,x_0)$ in the key-equations section.

6. Problem-Solving Tips

  1. Always define $\alpha_t=1-\beta_t$ and $\bar{\alpha}_t=\prod_i\alpha_i$ before deriving anything.
  2. Use reparameterization to derive $q(x_t\mid x_0)$; do not expand PDF products unless asked.
  3. For ELBO questions, name the three terms and their meanings.
  4. For posterior questions, write Bayes' rule and remember "product of Gaussians is Gaussian."
  5. For sampling questions, start from $x_T\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ and run reverse steps down to $x_0$.

7. Common Mistakes

  • Mixing $\alpha_t$ and $\bar{\alpha}_t$. One is per-step; one is cumulative.
  • Forgetting square roots in the state equation.
  • Thinking the forward process is learned. In the basic DDPM setup, the noising schedule is fixed.
  • Claiming exact density $p_\theta(x_0)$ is easy. It requires integrating out the full latent chain, so ELBO is used instead.
  • Confusing diffusion generation with LLM generation: diffusion refines the whole sample; LLMs generate left to right.

8. Exam Focus

  • Write $q(x_t\mid x_{t-1})$ and $q(x_t\mid x_0)$.
  • Explain why $x_T$ approaches $\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ when $\bar{\alpha}_T$ is small.
  • Write the ELBO three-term decomposition and explain each term.
  • Derive the posterior mean $\tilde{\mu}_t(x_t,x_0)$ or at least set up the Gaussian product correctly.
  • Describe inference: sample noise, then repeatedly denoise.

9. Quick Checklist

  • Forward process: add Gaussian noise.
  • Reverse process: learn denoising transitions.
  • $\alpha_t=1-\beta_t$, $\bar{\alpha}_t=\prod_i\alpha_i$.
  • $q(x_t\mid x_0)=\mathcal{N}(\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0,(1-\bar{\alpha}_t)I)$.
  • ELBO = reconstruction minus prior KL minus transition KLs.
  • Generation runs from $T$ down to $0$.

1. Concept Understanding

Diffusion model 是一种生成模型，核心是学习反转一个逐步加噪过程。Forward process 把 clean data $x_0$ 逐步变成接近标准高斯噪声 $x_T$；reverse process 从噪声开始，一步步 denoise 回样本。

Lecture 20 把 diffusion 看成 iterative VAE：VAE 只有一个 latent variable $z$，diffusion 则有一整条 latent noisy states：$x_1,\ldots,x_T$。

2. 大白话理解

想象你拿一张清晰照片，每次加一点点雪花噪声，重复很多次，最后它变成纯噪声。训练时，网络学的是“怎么把一步噪声往回擦掉”。生成时，就从随机噪声开始，一步一步擦回图片。

所以图像 diffusion 常像 coarse-to-fine refinement：前面几步先恢复大轮廓，后面几步再补细节。

3. Core Intuition

Forward 方向简单，因为是我们自己规定的：按 noise schedule 加 Gaussian noise。Reverse 方向难，因为同一个 noisy image 可能来自很多 clean images。神经网络要学一个逆向 Gaussian transition，常见实现会预测 noise $\epsilon$ 或 clean image $x_0$。

固定 forward process 给了可计算的训练信号；学到的 reverse process 就变成 sampler：$x_T\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)$，然后 $x_T\to x_{T-1}\to\cdots\to x_0$。

4. 核心公式 / Key Equations

符号表 / Notation

$x_0$ 是 clean data，$x_t$ 是第 $t$ 步 noisy state，$T$ 是最后几乎纯噪声的步数，$I$ 是 identity covariance matrix，$\beta_t$ 是噪声方差，$\alpha_t=1-\beta_t$，$\bar{\alpha}_t=\prod_{i=1}^t\alpha_i$。

如何读 diffusion 公式：$q(\cdot)$ 是 fixed forward noising process；$p_\theta(\cdot)$ 是 learned reverse denoising process。$\alpha_t,\beta_t$ 描述单步变化；$\bar{\alpha}_t$ 描述从 $0$ 到 $t$ 的累计效果。

怎么用这套符号：先判断题目是在 forward 加噪（$q$）还是 reverse 去噪（$p_\theta$）；再判断它问的是单步变化（$\alpha_t,\beta_t$）还是从 clean data 直接跳到第 $t$ 步（$\bar{\alpha}_t$）。

正向加噪 / Forward noising process

$$ q(x_t\mid x_{t-1})=\mathcal{N}\!\left(x_t;\sqrt{\alpha_t}\,x_{t-1},\,\beta_t I\right) $$
$$ x_t=\sqrt{\alpha_t}\,x_{t-1}+\sqrt{\beta_t}\,\epsilon_t,\qquad \epsilon_t\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I) $$

这表示：给定 previous noisy state $x_{t-1}$，下一步 $x_t$ 是 Gaussian。它的 mean 是把旧状态乘 $\sqrt{\alpha_t}$ 稍微缩小；covariance 是 $\beta_t I$，表示加入 variance 为 $\beta_t$ 的 isotropic Gaussian noise。

  • $\epsilon_t$ 是 fresh standard Gaussian noise。sample 一步时，先 draw $\epsilon_t$，再代入 reparameterized equation。
  • 做 ELBO / probability derivation 时用 distribution form；做 sampling 或推 $q(x_t\mid x_0)$ 时用 reparameterized form。

从 clean data 直接采样 / Direct sample from $x_0$

$$ q(x_t\mid x_0)=\mathcal{N}\!\left(x_t;\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}\,x_0,\,(1-\bar{\alpha}_t)I\right) $$

这是 shortcut formula。不需要真的模拟每个 intermediate step，可以从 clean data $x_0$ 直接跳到任意 noise level $t$：

$$ x_t=\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0+\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}\epsilon,\qquad \epsilon\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I). $$

$t$ 小时，$\bar{\alpha}_t$ 接近 $1$，所以 $x_t$ mostly clean signal；$t$ 大时，$\bar{\alpha}_t$ 接近 $0$，所以 $x_t$ mostly noise。

  • $\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}$ 是剩下的 signal scale，会缩小原始 clean sample。
  • $\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}$ 是 noise scale，累计加噪越多，它越大。
  • 训练代码题里通常是：sample $t$，sample $\epsilon$，构造 $x_t$，再让 network 预测 noise 或 clean signal。

学到的反向去噪 / Learned reverse transition

$$ p_\theta(x_{t-1}\mid x_t)=\mathcal{N}\!\left(x_{t-1};\mu_\theta(x_t,t),\,\Sigma_\theta(x_t,t)\right) $$

这是模型要学的部分。generation 时，从 $x_T\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ 开始，然后不断使用 $p_\theta(x_{t-1}\mid x_t)$ 从 $T$ reverse 到 $0$。

  • $\theta$ 是 neural-network parameters。
  • $\mu_\theta(x_t,t)$ 是模型预测的 slightly cleaner previous state 的 mean。
  • $\Sigma_\theta(x_t,t)$ 是 reverse-step covariance；很多简化推导里它会固定或用简单形式 parameterize。

HW4 §3.4 的 posterior mean

$$ \tilde{\mu}_t(x_t,x_0)= \frac{\sqrt{\alpha_t}(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1})}{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}x_t+ \frac{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}\beta_t}{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0 $$

这是 true posterior $q(x_{t-1}\mid x_t,x_0)$ 的 mean。它是 noisy observation $x_t$ 和 clean anchor $x_0$ 的 weighted average。推导时从 Bayes' rule 开始：

$$ q(x_{t-1}\mid x_t,x_0)\propto q(x_t\mid x_{t-1})q(x_{t-1}\mid x_0). $$

两个 factors 关于 variable $x_{t-1}$ 都是 Gaussian，所以乘积仍然是 Gaussian。对 exponent complete the square，就得到上面的 mean。

  • $x_t$ 前面的系数表示当前 noisy observation 对上一时刻的拉动有多强。
  • $x_0$ 前面的系数表示 clean reference 对 posterior 的 anchor 有多强。
  • 套公式时先算 $\alpha_t$、$\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}$、$\bar{\alpha}_t$、$\beta_t$，再把 $x_t$ 和 $x_0$ 代进去。

5. Worked Examples

HW4 §3.1：写 diffusion ELBO

出处：HW4 §3，题意转述。

$$ \log p_\theta(x_0)\ge \mathbb{E}_{q(x_1\mid x_0)}[\log p_\theta(x_0\mid x_1)] -D_{KL}(q(x_T\mid x_0)\,\|\,p(x_T)) -\sum_{t=2}^{T}\mathbb{E}_{q(x_t\mid x_0)} \left[D_{KL}(q(x_{t-1}\mid x_t,x_0)\,\|\,p_\theta(x_{t-1}\mid x_t))\right] $$

三项分别是 reconstruction、prior matching、step consistency。

HW4 §3.3：推 $q(x_t\mid x_0)$

反复代入 reparameterized transition。独立 Gaussian 的线性组合仍然是 Gaussian；信号系数一路相乘得到 $\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}$，剩下的噪声方差合并成 $1-\bar{\alpha}_t$。

HW4 §3.4：posterior mean

用 Bayes' rule：

$$ q(x_{t-1}\mid x_t,x_0)\propto q(x_t\mid x_{t-1})q(x_{t-1}\mid x_0) $$

两项都是关于 $x_{t-1}$ 的 Gaussian，乘积仍然是 Gaussian；配平方后得到 Key Equations 里的 $\tilde{\mu}_t(x_t,x_0)$。

6. Problem-Solving Tips

  1. 推导前先定义 $\alpha_t=1-\beta_t$ 和 $\bar{\alpha}_t=\prod_i\alpha_i$。
  2. 推 $q(x_t\mid x_0)$ 用 reparameterization，别上来就硬乘 PDF。
  3. ELBO 题先写三项，再解释每项含义。
  4. Posterior 题先写 Bayes' rule，并记住 product of Gaussians is Gaussian。
  5. Sampling 题从 $x_T\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ 开始，一步步 reverse 到 $x_0$。

7. Common Mistakes

  • 混淆 $\alpha_t$ 和 $\bar{\alpha}_t$：一个是单步，一个是累计。
  • 状态方程里忘记 square root。
  • 以为 forward process 是学出来的。基础 DDPM 里 noising schedule 是固定的。
  • 说可以轻易直接算 $p_\theta(x_0)$。它要积分掉整条 latent chain，所以通常用 ELBO。
  • 把 diffusion generation 和 LLM generation 混起来：diffusion 是整张样本反复 refine；LLM 是 left-to-right token 生成。

8. Exam Focus

  • 会写 $q(x_t\mid x_{t-1})$ 和 $q(x_t\mid x_0)$。
  • 能解释 $\bar{\alpha}_T$ 很小时，$x_T$ 为什么接近 $\mathcal{N}(0,I)$。
  • 会写 ELBO 三项分解，并解释三项。
  • 会推 posterior mean $\tilde{\mu}_t(x_t,x_0)$，至少能正确 setup Gaussian product。
  • 能描述 inference：sample noise，然后不断 denoise。

9. Quick Checklist

  • Forward process：加 Gaussian noise。
  • Reverse process：学习 denoising transitions。
  • $\alpha_t=1-\beta_t$，$\bar{\alpha}_t=\prod_i\alpha_i$。
  • $q(x_t\mid x_0)=\mathcal{N}(\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0,(1-\bar{\alpha}_t)I)$。
  • ELBO = reconstruction - prior KL - transition KLs。
  • 生成从 $T$ 到 $0$。
